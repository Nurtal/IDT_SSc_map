# Requalify the curation claims and write the Limitations paragraph

## What to build

The resource is described as community- and expert-curated, and its methodology claims scoping with
clinicians. No reaction has been ratified by anyone, the reviewer deck came back empty, and the
co-author brief was never answered.

This ticket makes every document say what is actually true, and puts the statement in exactly one
place — the Limitations paragraph. Said once it is rigour; repeated across four sections it reads as
an apology and invites the reader to discount the work.

This is the ticket that closes the lot: it is not done until the guard runs with an empty allowlist.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] No document asserts expert validation, expert curation or community curation of the map
- [ ] Claims describing steps that did not happen are corrected or removed rather than softened
- [ ] Exactly one Limitations paragraph states: curation by a single curator, no independent expert ratification to date, modules as an expert-defined reading frame
- [ ] That statement appears nowhere else — not in Results, not in the Discussion, not in the abstract
- [ ] The register is factual: no apology, no hedging beyond the fact, no repetition
- [ ] The reviewer app is framed as a mechanism offered for community adjudication, not as a step performed
- [ ] **The allowlist is empty** and the guard passes with no exemptions beyond the documented structural ones
